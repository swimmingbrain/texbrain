\section{Hello, world!}

This document was compiled right here in your browser. Change something on
the left and the PDF follows a moment after you stop typing. Ctrl+Enter
(Cmd+Enter on a Mac) compiles right away, Ctrl+S saves and compiles.

\section{Some math}

Everything pdfTeX can do works, for example
\begin{equation}
  x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}
\end{equation}
or inline math like $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$.

\section{Your own project}

Use \emph{Open Folder} to work on a project from your disk, \emph{New Project}
to start a fresh one, or clone a git repository. Your files stay on your
machine either way.
